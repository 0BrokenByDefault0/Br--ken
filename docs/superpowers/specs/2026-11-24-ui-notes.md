## Look
- **Single dark look.** The app is dark in both system themes: a night studio vibe, with no light mode in Phase 1.
- **Palette:**

  | Token | Hex | Used for |
  |---|---|---|
  | night | #15131f | background |
  | panel | #1f1c2e | panels |
  | haze | #c8b8ff | primary accent (lilac) |
  | amber | #ffb547 | "you're here", playhead, live/playing |
  | tone | #6fe3b4 | chord tone, pass, success |
  | color | #8fb4ff | color tone |
  | clash | #ff7a6b | clash, fail |
  | ink | #ecebf5 | text |
  | mute | #8d89a6 | secondary text |

- **Type:** use the system fonts in the app.
  - SF Pro Rounded or SF Pro Display semibold for titles and stage numbers.
  - SF Pro for body text.
  - SF Mono for note names, numbers and chord symbols.

  This keeps the bundle small and needs no font licensing.
- **Touch targets** are 44 pt or larger. Press states scale to about 0.96, with a light haptic on hits, toggles and "Use".

## Navigation
- **Bottom tab bar:** Journey · Lesson (the current or last lesson) · Chords (Chord Finder on Your Track's melody) · Drums (drum grid on Your Track's drums). The tools are also reachable inside lessons.
- **Journey map** is a vertical timeline with numbered nodes:
  - done: filled lilac
  - you're here: amber ring with a slow pulse and a "you're here" pill
  - not started: outline
  - coming later: dashed and muted
- Tapping a stage expands its lessons inline. The next lesson gets an amber "Start" pill.
- **Your Track card** sits at the top of the map:
  - play/stop button
  - a 3-row mini timeline (melody / chords / drums) with a playhead
  - part chips that mute or unmute on tap; a dashed chip means the part isn't built yet
  - a meta line: key · BPM · bars

## Lesson player
- **Progress row:** five labeled dots (Idea · Hear it · Do it · Keep it · Koala). Dots are tappable to jump.
- **Bottom bar:** a sticky "Back" button plus "Next: <step name>". The last step's button reads "Finish lesson".
- **Idea step:** a lead sentence, then visual swatches for the concept (e.g. chord tone / color / clash), then a "Go deeper" disclosure.
- **Hear it:** A/B cards with a large letter, a tag (Fits / Clashes), the progression, a one-line explanation, a play button, and a progress bar along the bottom edge.
- **Do it:** the tool inline, then "Check my chords" (the label varies by task). Results appear as ✓/✕ rows with specific messages.
- **Keep it:** "Saved to Your Track" with the result and a playable Your Track lane. If the owner skipped a pick, it says what was auto-filled.
- **Koala:** a numbered mission (about 3 steps, ~5 min) and an amber "Export loop to Koala" button that opens the export sheet.

## Chord Finder
- **Slot strip** at the top (Bar 1 | Bar 2) with a play-all button. Slots show the chord and its roman numeral.
- **Melody bars:** note chips sit on a 16-step grid per bar. Each chip is colored by its role under the selected or used chord, and shows its interval label (R, ♭3, 5, 9…).
- **Legend:** chord tone / color tone / clash.
- **Bar selector:** a segmented control. Each bar lists its ranked fits:
  - chord symbol plus roman numeral
  - the chord's notes
  - a score meter colored by score band: ≥70% of best = tone color, ≥40% = color, below that = clash
  - a "Use" button
- Tapping a fit auditions it under that bar and shows a one-line reason. Choosing bar 1 moves focus to bar 2.
- **Scores** are live from `ChordFinder` in the app, shown 0–100 relative to the bar's best fit.

## Drum grid
- Transport (play/stop, swing slider), then a Draw | Roll segmented control.
- **Roll mode** shows rate (1/32, 1/16T, 1/32T, 1/64) and ramp (Up / Flat / Down) options. Dragging across hat steps creates a roll, and roll cells show subdivision ticks scaled by velocity.
- Beat columns are shaded; a playhead column is highlighted in amber.
- **Phone width:** the grid shows one bar of 16 steps. Steps narrow to fit 16 at about 390 pt, falling back to horizontal scroll inside the grid on small phones.
- **Pads:** a 3×2 grid under the drum grid for finger drumming.
- **"Save this bar to Your Track"** applies when the grid is used outside a lesson.
